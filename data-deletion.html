Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 2026-06-06

Learn Vocabulary lets you request deletion of the data associated with your use of the app. We do not require you to create an account, but if any data is linked to you (for example, an account identifier or analytics tied to your device), you can ask us to delete it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@learnvocabulary.io.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or account identifier you used in the app, so we can locate your data.

What happens next

We will confirm your request and delete the associated data within 30 days. We may retain a limited amount of data where required by law or to prevent abuse.

Contact

Email: support@learnvocabulary.io
